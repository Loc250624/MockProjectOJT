Support guidance

No support phone number, email address, or service-level promise is configured in this application. Use the official support channel provided by your institution or platform administrator.

For platform configuration concerns, verify settings and logs through existing admin tools before escalating.

For course or student access issues, collect the course name, learner account, and page where the issue occurs.

For enrollment, payment, or course access issues, keep the course name and any visible transaction or result status ready.
