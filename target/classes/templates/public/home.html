Frequently Asked Questions

Can I access courses offline? ▼
Yes! Scholar and Institutional plan members can download lessons for offline viewing through the LumiNa mobile app.
Are certificates recognized by employers? ▼
LumiNa certificates are backed by partner institutions and recognized by leading employers globally. Each certificate includes a verifiable credential ID.
How do live sessions work? ▼
Live sessions are scheduled by instructors and accessible through the portal. All sessions are recorded and available for 30 days after the event.

Yes! Register as a Teacher, complete our instructor verification process, and start building courses using our Curriculum Builder tool.
