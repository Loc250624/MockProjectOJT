Course Overview

This intensive advanced-level course provides a comprehensive roadmap for understanding how quantum mechanics is redefining the landscape of digital security. You will transition from classical RSA/ECC foundations to the complex world of qubits, superposition, and quantum key distribution (QKD).

Designed for researchers and security professionals, this curriculum focuses on the technical rigor required to architect post-quantum resistant systems.

What you'll learn

check_circle The physical principles of Qubits and Quantum Entanglement.
check_circle Vulnerabilities of RSA and Elliptic Curve in a quantum world.
check_circle Implementation of BB84 Protocol for secure key exchange.
check_circle Lattice-based and Code-based Post-Quantum Cryptography.
